# Soundness Audit Report — `nico/update_multi_parallel_fold_support`

## Verdict

The uncommitted change set is **sound against SuperNeo (Sec 4/5, Thms 3–5, Defs 7–8) and HyperNova**, and is in fact a **net strengthening** of carried-digest authority. The headline semantic move — replacing the commitment-only accumulator handle (`accumulator_digest_from_parent_claim`, which bound only `parent.c.data`) with `accumulator_digest_from_running_parts`, binding every running CE claim's full public fields (`c, X, r, s_col, y_ring, ct, y_zcol, m_in, fold_digest`) plus the Π_RLC parent — closes a real DIGEST-AS-NON-AUTHORITY gap and is verified byte-for-byte parity native↔circuit. All performance rewrites (Toom-3 in-circuit ring mul, ring-rotation RLC fold, signed-unit/binary Ajtai fast paths, optimized Gram, parallel CSC/tensor_point, zero-digit-plane DEC skip) were cross-checked numerically against their canonical references with zero mismatches. The carried `acc_digest` is never trusted as authority (recomputed from opened witnesses), the five CE obligations are preserved in both sequential and shared-r parallel branches, and hashing is Poseidon2-only throughout. **There are zero critical or high soundness findings.** Nothing blocks merge on soundness grounds. **One medium build-break (NR-1) on the wasm32-no-threads target must be fixed before that target can compile**; it is not a soundness defect. Remaining items are coverage gaps and drift/maintainability hygiene.

## Critical & High

**None.** No verifier-accepts-invalid-proof, no extractor break, no binding break was found across any subsystem.
